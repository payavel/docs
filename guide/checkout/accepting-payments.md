# Accepting Payments

Payavel Checkout is the dedicated package for integrating, processing, and managing payments within your Laravel application.

Whether you are capturing one-time payments, authorizing funds, or handling complex multi-gateway payment flows, Checkout provides an elegant API to securely interface with any payment provider (such as Stripe, Adyen, Braintree, and more) without tying your application's logic to any single vendor.

Built on top of Payavel's [Orchestration](../orchestration/getting-started) layer, Checkout entirely abstracts away the distinct nuances and specific implementation details of your underlying gateways. By decoupling your payment workflows from the providers powering them, you can build scalable, incredibly flexible payment infrastructure that can easily adapt as your business requirements evolve.
