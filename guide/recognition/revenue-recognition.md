# Revenue Recognition

> [!WARNING] Coming Soon
> **Recognition** is an upcoming package in our end-to-end commerce suite, specifically focused on **financial reporting and data reconciliation**.

This package consolidates financial data from multiple payment, billing, and order management providers, simplifying the processes of revenue recognition, reconciliation, and broader financial reporting through a unified API.

Here is an overview of what the Recognition package features:

- **Provider-Agnostic Interface**: Seamlessly integrate and pull data from various payment and billing providers without altering your core application logic.
- **Data Consolidation**: Centralize your financial data to create a single source of truth for revenue across your entire ecosystem.
- **Automated Reconciliation**: Effortlessly match and reconcile transactions, payouts, and fees across disparate systems.
- **Financial Reporting**: Generate accurate, real-time reports to streamline revenue recognition and simplify accounting workflows.
