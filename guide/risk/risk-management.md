# Risk Management

> [!WARNING] Coming Soon
> **Risk** is an upcoming package in our end-to-end commerce suite, specifically focused on **fraud prevention and risk mitigation**.

This package is designed to protect your business with built-in fraud prevention integrations that shield your revenue stream. It abstracts the integration with various risk evaluation APIs, allowing you to establish a robust and provider-agnostic defense against chargebacks and fraudulent behavior.

Here is an overview of what the Risk package will feature:

- **Provider-Agnostic Interface**: Seamlessly integrate and switch between different risk evaluation APIs without altering your core application logic.
- **Risk Assessment Engine**: Dynamically flag, block, or approve transactions based on customizable risk thresholds and rules.
- **Automated Fraud Protection**: Shield your revenue stream by identifying and neutralizing malicious activity and behaviors in real time.
- **Chargeback Mitigation**: Streamline the process of managing and defending against chargebacks with consolidated data and logs.
